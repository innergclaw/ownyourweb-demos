# Need a Hand

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated to the existing OwnYourWeb GitHub Pages demo repository: static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Users

People in Philadelphia who need help with small household tasks. The supplied flyer mentions elders, busy parents, and families.

## Product Purpose

Show the available help and turn a short service request into a text addressed to 267-473-0397. A request is not a confirmed booking.

## Operating Context

The visitor enters a first name, phone number, Philadelphia area, service request, preferred day and time, whether the work is indoors or outside, and whether tools are available. The visitor reviews the prepared message and sends it in their own messaging app.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The page is static and does not transmit or store form data.
- The messaging app must be available on the visitor's device. The visitor must tap Send there.
- The owner has not supplied prices, availability, service limits, a business address, or a booking system. Do not invent them.
- Phone number supplied by the user: 267-473-0397.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the supplied flyer as the factual and visual reference.
- Working name from the flyer: Need a Hand. This remains open to correction by the user.
- Keep the flyer’s navy, white, and yellow cues, household imagery, and direct tone.

## Evidence on Hand

- User-supplied flyer: `assets/need-a-hand-flyer.jpg`.
- Listed help: lifting and carrying, moving items from basements, closets, and storage, furniture setup, light household help, and support for elders, busy parents, and families.
- The flyer mentions flexible scheduling but provides no posted hours.

## Product Principles

- Make the service scope clear before the form.
- Use the exact request fields supplied by the user.
- Explain that the customer sends the message and waits for confirmation.
- Keep the page usable without a backend or payment account.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, clear labels, keyboard controls, mobile-friendly inputs, visible focus, and plain instructions for devices that do not prefill SMS text.
