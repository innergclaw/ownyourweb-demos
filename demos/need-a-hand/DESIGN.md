# Need a Hand design handoff

The supplied 1024×1280 flyer sets the visual direction. Its left side is a household scene with handwritten notes. Its right side uses a compact navy service headline, small yellow accents, and a navy footer. The page uses the same navy, white, and yellow family without copying the crowded flyer layout.

The first screen states the service and shows the flyer. The next section explains the types of help. The final section collects the requested details, then shows the exact text for review before opening Messages. The page does not report a booking or claim automatic delivery.

The page uses Barlow Condensed for headings and Manrope for body text. It has a desktop two-column hero and a single-column mobile flow. Motion is limited to the hero entrance and button hover, and is removed when the visitor requests reduced motion.
