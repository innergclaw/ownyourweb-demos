05 / Booking FAQ

Before you book.

How does the Back-to-School Special work?

Book three children together under the special. Two kids receive paid haircuts and the third child’s haircut is free. Final eligibility and appointment details are confirmed by the barbershop.

Can I book a regular adult appointment?

Yes. Choose “Regular Appointment” in the booking section, then select Adult Haircut, Haircut + Beard, Lineup / Edge-up, or another service.

Are walk-ins accepted?

Walk-ins are welcome, but families using the Back-to-School Special should book ahead so enough time can be reserved for all children.

Is the appointment confirmed after submitting?

Not yet. The live system will check barber availability and send a confirmation by text or email. This demo does not transmit or reserve appointments.
