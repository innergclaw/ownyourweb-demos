04 / Parent FAQ

Questions before game day?

How are teams selected?

Little Ballers is a draft league. Registrations are organized by the league to create balanced teams and a fair experience for young players.

Does my child need basketball experience?

No. First-time players are welcome. The age 4–6 division focuses on fundamentals, confidence, movement, teamwork, and enjoying the game.

When and where are games?

Games are scheduled for Saturday mornings at St. Benedict Church, 1940 E. Chelten Avenue in Philadelphia. Use the entrance on Opal Street.

How do I pay the $65 registration fee?

The league accepts Apple Pay, Zelle, Cash App, and cash. A league organizer will confirm the correct payment details after your registration is received.

Can I register more than one child?

Yes. Complete a separate registration form for each child so player profiles, emergency contacts, and team placement remain accurate.
