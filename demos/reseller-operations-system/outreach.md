OwnYourWeb Systems helps growing resellers replace disconnected spreadsheets and repeated manual entry with one back-office workflow connecting inventory, sales, payouts, expenses, and profit.

## Follow-up after 3–5 days

Just following up in case the reseller workflow map would still be useful. Even if you decide not to build anything yet, it should clarify what can be automated, what should stay manual, and which duplicate-entry step is worth fixing first.

## 15-minute qualification call

1. What makes an item unique in your current system: SKU, serial number, item number, or description?
2. Where is the original purchase cost stored?
3. Which channels generate the most completed sales each month?
4. Which payment accounts receive marketplace or direct-sale money?
5. What information must be ready for your bookkeeper or taxes?
6. How often do you discover a sale, fee, or deposit that cannot be matched?
7. Who besides you needs access, and what should they be allowed to see or change?
8. Which single manual step would save the most time if fixed first?

## Guardrails

- Never call the Reddit poster an existing client.
- Never claim a result that has not happened.
- Do not ask for passwords or raw bank credentials.
- Confirm Chrono24 account/export access before promising automation.
- Treat ambiguous matches as review tasks, not automatic truth.
- Use secure user authorization, client-scoped permissions, and audit logs in a production build.
