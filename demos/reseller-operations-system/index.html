02 / Scenario-based case study

A vintage-watch reseller outgrew manual entry.

This anonymized scenario reflects a common reseller problem: a viable part-time business operating across marketplaces, direct payments, and in-person sales with no connected back office.

Before the system

  • Each transaction is entered by hand.
  • Inventory and sales reports disagree.
  • Marketplace fees hide true item profit.
  • Bank deposits are difficult to trace.
  • Growth adds administrative chaos.

What we would build

  • One SKU and digital record for every item.
  • Order imports for supported marketplaces.
  • Secure transaction feeds and CSV fallbacks.
  • A reconciliation queue for uncertain matches.
  • Real net profit after cost, fees, and shipping.
  • Bookkeeping-ready monthly exports and audit history.

Illustrative case study only. This is not presented as a completed client engagement and contains no claimed business results.
